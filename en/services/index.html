Develop in a regulated environment

Quality and regulatory requirements integrated from the design stage, with documentation and tests suited to the system being developed.

  • Formalization of functional requirements (URS / FRS).
  • Risk analysis and requirements traceability.
  • Preparation of the technical documentation and of elements of the validation package.
  • Design and execution of functional tests.
  • Application of GAMP 5 and ALCOA+ principles, taking into account the applicable requirements of ISO 13485 and 21 CFR Part 11.

Activities are carried out within the agreed scope and according to the client’s quality procedures. Validation and approval of the system remain the responsibility of the authorized functions at the client.

Govern the project and control compliance

Structuring of technical and documentation follow-up to clarify responsibilities, control changes and ensure the traceability of decisions.

  • Clarification of roles and responsibilities.
  • Management of change requests (Change Requests, CR).
  • Tracking of versions and deliverables.
  • Traceability of requirements, decisions and tests.

The aim: to adapt the level of documentation, testing and validation to the risks and the impact of changes, without needlessly burdening processes.

Leverage industrial data

Development of analysis and decision-support tools, automation of data processing and prototyping of data-driven or artificial-intelligence solutions, according to process needs.

Scopes of work

  • Targeted development: building a feature, an application or an interface.
  • Complete technical work package: handling of development, integration, testing and documentation.
  • Extension of existing solutions: adaptation and deployment of applications across several machines or production lines.
  • Systems integration: interconnection of applications, equipment and management tools.

The scope, deliverables, interfaces with the teams and acceptance criteria are defined upfront.

From requirement to go-live

  1. Understand the need
    Analyze the process, the operational constraints, the functional requirements and the existing technical environment.
  2. Design the solution
    Define the features, the architecture and the data exchanges.
  3. Develop and integrate
    Build the applications and interfaces, then connect them to the relevant systems and equipment.
  4. Test and document
    Verify operation, ensure the traceability of developments and prepare the elements needed for control and validation of the system.
  5. Prepare deployment
    Support go-live, technical handover and extension of the solution within the agreed scope.
